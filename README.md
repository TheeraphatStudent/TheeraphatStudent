### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a full-stack developer with **5 years and 4 months of experience** designing and building reliable, user-focused web applications end-to-end. On the front end, I craft intuitive interfaces with **Angular**, **Next.js**, and **Tailwind CSS**; on the back end, I build scalable, efficient APIs and services with **NestJS** and **FastAPI**. Beyond the web, my work spans mobile development with **Flutter**, AI/automation tooling with **Python**, **n8n**, and **Roboflow**, and cloud & DevOps work across **GCP**, **AWS**, **Docker**, and **Kubernetes**.

Over the past few years I've shipped a quality-control assessment platform for supermarket operations (Makro & Lotus's), led end-to-end UX/UI design for the U Work app, built an animation-rich site for The Horotoday, and most recently designed and built a full-stack field-reporting platform for fumigation technicians (SGT Fumigation), alongside ongoing QA and backend work for Morya. I'm committed to staying current with emerging technologies and delivering high-quality code that meets real project requirements.

- 🔭 **Currently building:** a fumigation reporting platform for field technicians (SGT Fumigation)
- 🌱 **Currently exploring:** AI automation with n8n, Roboflow & Ollama
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Angular, Next.js, NestJS, FastAPI, Flutter
- 📍 **Based in:** Chaiyaphum, Thailand
